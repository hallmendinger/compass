    Budget overview

    Each field of the form opens on its own. On the left, what is for us: notes, open decisions, what a field is waiting for. On the right, the application text as a reviewer will read it.

    A circled question mark is an open decision; open it to answer. A circled P marks a proposal. The text on the right is edited by one person at a time — whoever presses “edit” holds the field until they save or cancel, and every earlier version is kept.

    Each work package has its own symbol — a clipboard for project management, two cards for card sorting, a die for the stochastic quiz, a magnifying glass for evaluation, waves for dissemination, and a magnifying glass with waves for evaluation and transfer. The symbol stands wherever the work package appears: in the Gantt chart, the matrix, the budget and the form.

    The form shows the character limit of each field only once it is open online; until then, each field counts its characters.
